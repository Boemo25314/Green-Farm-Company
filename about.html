Our Purpose

Why We Do What We Do

Green Farm Agriculture was born from a simple belief: every family deserves access to food that is genuinely fresh, grown with integrity, and fairly priced. When our founder, Mr. Goitseone Kgosi, started planting his first vegetable beds in 2005, he had one goal in mind to feed his community well.

Today, that vision has grown into a full farm operation covering over 15 hectares, with a dedicated team of 12 farm workers, a daily delivery service, and hundreds of satisfied customers across Botswana. The heart of the business has not changed though we still do things the right way.

Our Mission

To provide fresh, natural food directly from our farm to every household in Botswana.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and sustainable agriculture brand in Southern Africa.

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

Every decision we make is rooted in these principles.

Organic Farming

We use only natural compost and organic pest control methods. No harmful chemical.

Ethical Animal Care

Our animals live in open, clean spaces with plenty of food, water, and sunshine. Happy animals produce better food.

Water Conservation

We use drip irrigation and rainwater harvesting to protect Botswana's precious water resources.

Community First

We hire locally, train young farmers, and give back to the communities around us.
